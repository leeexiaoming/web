I am an Assistant Professor of Information Technology and Business Analytics in the [Department of Information Technology, Analytics & Operations](https://poole.ncsu.edu/academic-departments/information-technology-analytics-and-operations/) at NC State University's Poole College of Management.

My research examines the economic and managerial questions created by digital platforms. I study how people and organizations participate in open innovation, crowdsourcing and online labor markets, as well as how social media and AI-enabled services shape stakeholder behavior and welfare.
